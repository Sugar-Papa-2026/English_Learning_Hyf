# 《老友记》S1E14 生词与台词整理

1. **bail on me** — bail /beɪl/ v. 放鸽子、临时丢下、临阵脱逃。原句：Don't you bail on me. 补充：≠ betray（背叛）。
2. **vulnerable** /ˈvʌlnərəbl/ adj. 脆弱的，容易受伤的。原句：Come on, she's needy, vulnerable. 补充：needy 指情感缺爱、黏人。
3. **trading** /ˈtreɪdɪŋ/ — trade 的 -ing 形式；交换、调换。原句：No trading. You get the pretty one, I get the mess. 补充：the mess 指难搞定的那个。
4. **cab** /kæb/ n. 出租车（美式口语 = taxi）；cab smell：出租车里的味道。原句：I'm gonna wash the cab smell off my hands.
5. **puppet** /ˈpʌpɪt/ n. 木偶；hand puppet 手偶。原句：You could make little puppet out of them.
6. **theatre of cruelty** — 残酷戏剧（先锋戏剧术语）。原句：And you could use them in your theater of cruelty.
7. **slather** /ˈslæðə(r)/ v. 厚厚地涂抹；slather A with B：把 B 厚厚涂在 A 上。原句：She said she wants to slather my body with stuff and lick it off.
8. **zipper** /ˈzɪpə(r)/ n. 拉链；stick out：露出来、伸出来。原句：That your shirt's been sticking out of your zipper.
9. **Rob Roy** — 罗伯·罗伊，经典苏格兰威士忌鸡尾酒。原句：That's right. Each a Rob Roy.
10. **jet lag** /ˈdʒet læɡ/ n. 时差反应。原句：I'm just saying, if dogs experience jet lag.
11. **sage branches** — sage /seɪdʒ/ n. 鼠尾草；鼠尾草枝（民俗烟熏净化）。sacramental /ˌsækrəˈmentl/ adj. 圣礼的；sacramental wine 圣餐酒。原句：Now we need sage branches and the sacramental wine. 补充：Sacramento 是加州首府萨克拉门托。
12. **oregano** /ɒˈreɡɑːnəʊ/ n. 牛至；Fresca /ˈfreskə/ n. 柑橘味无糖气泡饮料。原句：All I have is oregano and a Fresca.
13. **ritual** /ˈrɪtʃuəl/ n. 仪式、流程；in the first place：压根、一开始。原句：If we had that, we wouldn't be doing the ritual in the first place.
14. **grappa** /ˈɡræpə/ n. 格拉帕酒，意大利果渣白兰地。原句：Here's Paul's grappa.
15. **matchmaker** /ˈmætʃmeɪkə(r)/ n. 媒人、红娘。原句：Hello, Joey, our little matchmaker.
16. **originally** /əˈrɪdʒənəli/ adv. 起初、原本。原句：Not originally. 补充：origin 起源；original 原本的。
17. **maxi pads** — 加长卫生巾；mini pads：迷你护垫；a piece of cake：小菜一碟。原句：The maxi pads were a piece of cake. Mini pads should be that much easier.
18. **scooch** /skuːtʃ/ v. 小幅度挪动；scooch down：挪一挪。原句：You guys will scooch down.
19. **hairy** /ˈheəri/ adj. 多毛的；chief /tʃiːf/ n. 长官、队长（这里指消防队长）。原句：That guy's hairier than the Chief!
20. **ventilated** /ˈventɪleɪtɪd/ adj. 通风的；well ventilated area：通风良好的地方。原句：Make sure you are in a well ventilated area.
21. **synthetic** /sɪnˈθetɪk/ adj. 合成的、人造的；fabric /ˈfæbrɪk/ n. 布料；synthetic fabrics 化纤面料。原句：If you wanna burn his clothing, stay away from synthetic fabrics.
22. **foghorn** /ˈfɔːɡhɔːrn/ n. 雾号、雾笛。原句：Something deep in your soul calls out to me like a foghorn.
23. **wolverine** /ˌwʊlvəˈriːn/ n. 狼獾、貂熊。原句：I never called your mother a wolverine. 补充：werewolf 是狼人。
24. **wacky** /ˈwæki/ adj. 古怪有趣的、脑洞离谱的。原句：Here's a wacky thought.
25. **stick a pin in it** — 先搁置、回头再说。原句：Let's just stick a pin in it.
26. **pole** /poʊl/ n. 杆子；slide down a pole：顺杆子滑下（消防滑杆）。原句：So you guys just slide down a pole.
27. **truck** /trʌk/ n. 卡车；本剧指 fire truck 消防车。原句：So, will you bring the truck?
28. **cleansing** /ˈklenzɪŋ/ n. 净化（仪式层面）；cleanse v. 净化。原句：The cleansing worked.
29. **aura** /ˈɔːrə/ n. 气场、灵光。原句：Stop cleansing my aura.
